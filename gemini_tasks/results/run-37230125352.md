## 2. Beginner-Level Inconsistencies

1. **Missing Excel File**:
   * `README.md` lists `sales_analysis_beginner.xlsx` under its `## Files` section, but this file is absent from the repository.
2. **Data & Layout Mismatches in `sales_dashboard_reference.svg`**:
   * **Phantom Product**: The SVG lists `2. Laptop Stand` in the Top Products visual, but "Laptop Stand" does not exist in `sales_data.csv` (the actual top products are Wireless Mouse, Keyboard, and Headphones).
   * **Inverted City Rankings & Missing Data**: The SVG displays Giza above Cairo with a larger bar, whereas `sales_data.csv` shows Cairo generated higher revenue (32,910 EGP) than Giza (28,110 EGP). Additionally, Qalyubia (19,250 EGP) is omitted from the chart.
   * **Layout Spec Discrepancy**: `POWER_BI_DESIGN.md` specifies "Revenue by Month and Revenue by Category in the middle", but the SVG displays "Revenue by City" instead of monthly performance.
3. **Missing Monthly Aggregations in Code**:
   * While monthly tracking is referenced in `POWER_BI_GUIDE.md` and `NOTES.md`, neither `analysis.py` nor `analysis.sql` includes month-over-month aggregation logic.
