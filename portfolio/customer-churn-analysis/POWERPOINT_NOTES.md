# PowerPoint Content

The automated deck contains:
1. Title and business question
2. Dataset and scope
3. KPI summary
4. Churn by plan
5. Churn by city
6. Churn by channel
7. Revenue by plan
8. Key observations and limitations
9. Conclusion and next steps

All figures are calculated from customer_data.csv during the build; no KPI values are hard-coded in the generator.
