# Power BI Design

## Style

Keep the dashboard clean, calm and easy to read.

Suggested colors:
- Navy: #0F172A
- Teal: #0F766E
- Cyan: #06B6D4
- Background: #F8FAFC

## Layout

Page: Sales Overview

1. Four KPI cards at the top.
2. Revenue by Month and Revenue by Category in the middle.
3. Revenue by City and Top Products at the bottom.

Avoid:
- 3D charts
- Gauges
- Too many colors
- Too many visuals on one page

The goal is a simple beginner dashboard, not an advanced BI report.
